# Demo Script — Dental Clinic

## Opening (30 seconds)
"Hello, and thank you for taking the time to see our AI voice assistant in action. I'm going to simulate a real patient calling your dental clinic after hours. This is exactly what would happen if a potential patient calls at 8 PM on a Saturday."

## Live Demo (3-5 minutes)

### Scenario 1: New Patient Booking
1. Dial the demo number
2. AI answers: "Hello, thank you for calling [Clinic Name]. This is Sarah, your virtual assistant. How can I help you today?"
3. You (as patient): "Hi, I'd like to book a cleaning appointment."
4. AI: "Of course, I'd be happy to help you with that. Are you a new patient or an existing patient?"
5. You: "I'm new."
6. AI: "Welcome! We'd love to see you. Can I have your full name, please?"
7. You: "John Smith."
8. AI: "Thank you, John. What's the best phone number to reach you?"
9. You: "555-123-4567."
10. AI: "And your email address?"
11. You: "john@example.com."
12. AI: "Great. Do you have dental insurance?"
13. You: "Yes, Delta Dental."
14. AI: "Perfect, we accept Delta Dental. Let me check our availability for you."
15. AI: [Checks calendar] "I have Thursday at 2:00 PM or Friday at 10:00 AM available for a 60-minute new patient visit. Which works better for you?"
16. You: "Thursday at 2."
17. AI: "Excellent, I've booked you for Thursday at 2:00 PM. You'll receive a confirmation text and email shortly. Is there anything else I can help you with?"
18. You: "No, that's all."
19. AI: "Thank you for calling, John. We look forward to seeing you on Thursday!"

### Scenario 2: FAQ + Upsell (2 minutes)
1. You: "How much does teeth whitening cost?"
2. AI: "Professional in-office whitening is $349, and we currently have a summer special for $299. Would you like to add it to your Thursday appointment?"
3. You: "Maybe, I'll think about it."
4. AI: "No problem at all. I'll make a note in your file. You can decide at your appointment. Is there anything else I can help with?"

### Scenario 3: Emergency Triage (1 minute)
1. You: "I have a terrible toothache and my face is swollen."
2. AI: "I'm sorry to hear that. A swollen face with tooth pain can be serious. We have an emergency slot available tomorrow at 8:00 AM. Can you come in then?"
3. You: "Yes."
4. AI: "I've reserved that spot for you. In the meantime, apply a cold compress and take ibuprofen if you can. If the swelling spreads or you have trouble breathing, please go to the ER immediately."

## Closing (1 minute)
"As you saw, the AI handled booking, FAQ, upsell, and emergency triage — all while your office was closed. Every call was answered, every lead captured, and every appointment confirmed. This runs 24/7, integrates with your existing systems, and costs less than one part-time receptionist."
