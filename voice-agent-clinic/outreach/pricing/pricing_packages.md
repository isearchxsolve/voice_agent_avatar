## ROI Calculator

```
Monthly Value = (Calls Answered × Conversion Rate × Avg Deal Value) + (Staff Hours Saved × Hourly Rate)

Example Dental Clinic:
- 300 calls/month, 70% answer rate → 210 calls answered
- 30% booking rate → 63 appointments booked
- Avg patient value $450 → $28,350 revenue
- 20 staff hours saved @ $25/hr → $500 savings
- Total monthly value: $28,850
- Cost: $599
- Net ROI: ($28,850 - $599) / $599 = 4,714%
```
